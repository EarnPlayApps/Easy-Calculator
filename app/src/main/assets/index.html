Privacy Policy

Easy Calculator · EarnPlayApps

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Easy Calculator is a calculator application by EarnPlayApps. The app does not require an account and is designed to perform calculations on the device.

Data handled by Easy Calculator

  • Calculator inputs and calculation history are processed/stored locally on the device and are not uploaded by Easy Calculator to a developer server.
  • The app does not request your name, contacts, phone number, precise location, photos, microphone or camera.
  • Health calculator inputs such as age, height and weight are used only to calculate the requested estimate on the device.

Advertising and third-party services

Easy Calculator uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google and its advertising partners may process device, advertising, usage, diagnostics and consent-related information as described in their policies. Ad requests may use information such as IP address, device identifiers and approximate location for advertising, security, measurement and related purposes.

Consent

Where consent is required, UMP is used to present the applicable consent choices. Users can manage available privacy choices through the app's Privacy Options entry when provided by Google.

History and deletion

Calculation history is stored locally. You can delete individual history entries or clear all history from the History screen. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data according to Android device behavior.

Health information disclaimer

Health calculators are informational estimates only and are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Do not rely on the results for medical decisions; consult a qualified healthcare professional when appropriate.

Financial and other calculator disclaimer

Money, tax, loan, business, construction and other calculations are estimates based on the values and assumptions entered. Verify important results with the relevant official source or qualified professional before making consequential decisions.

Children

Easy Calculator is not designed specifically for children. The target audience and advertising settings must be declared accurately in Google Play Console. If the target audience is changed to include children, additional Google Play Families requirements apply.

Privacy contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact information published with the Easy Calculator listing on Google Play. The privacy policy and Data safety information must be kept consistent with the actual data practices and third-party SDKs used by the app.

External policies

Google's own privacy, advertising and consent policies apply to Google's services integrated into the app. Easy Calculator does not control third-party data processing.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app, SDKs, advertising configuration or legal requirements change.
